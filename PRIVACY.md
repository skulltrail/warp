# Privacy

warp does not collect telemetry, analytics, or personal data.

The extension reads clicked download URLs and sends requested downloads directly to the qBittorrent or SABnzbd endpoint configured by the user. Backend credentials and activity logs remain in browser-local storage. Known credential-like URL parameters are redacted from logs.

Broad site access is required to intercept magnet, torrent, and NZB links across indexers. Data is not sent to the project maintainers or any third-party service.
